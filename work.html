Sonke Gender Justice · Sency Community
Understanding Consent

A collaborative awareness film produced with Sonke Gender Justice and the Sency Community, breaking consent down into plain, accessible language. Community voices and documentary footage were shaped into a short-form piece built for advocacy screenings and social distribution.
Sency Community Based Organisation
The Price of a Click

A digital-safety advocacy film for Sency Community Based Organisation, exploring the real cost of online risk for young people. Narrative and testimonial footage were cut into a campaign-ready piece for community screenings and online outreach.
Sarova Hotels
Sarova at 50

A milestone brand film marking fifty years of Kenyan hospitality, tracing the group's story through the people who built it. Interviews, archive and location cinematography were graded into a single celebratory narrative built for broadcast, events and social.
Lake Turkana Wind Power
LTWP visitor promo

A corporate promo film for Africa's largest wind power project, translating a complex energy operation into a clear, human story. Aerial and ground cinematography captured in demanding field conditions were cut into a visitor-ready introduction to the site and its impact.
Magical Kenya
Magical Kenya Ladies Open

Tournament coverage built for same-day broadcast and social distribution, keeping fans on the action as it happened. Multi-camera capture, rapid edits and branded graphics delivered highlights and full-length assets across the length of the event.
