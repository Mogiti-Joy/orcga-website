Rigorous protocols for participant consent and data privacy. We ensure individuals understand how their stories will be used, adhering to GDPR and local protection laws.

Strict adherence to PSEA standards. Our teams are trained to maintain the highest moral and ethical conduct in the field.

Ensuring subjects are portrayed with agency and respect, avoiding stereotypes and focusing on resilience and dignity.
